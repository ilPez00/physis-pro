## Inventory (RUNG 3 design)

`cargo public-api` is not installable here and rustdoc JSON needs nightly
(the workspace root defeats the one old nightly on disk), so the snapshot
uses the packet-sanctioned doc-grep: `tests/api_stability.rs` walks
`physis-core/src/**/*.rs`, tracks `pub mod` + struct/enum/trait/impl bodies
by brace depth, and records every `pub` item as
`path::Item — kind signature` (multi-line signatures joined, whitespace
normalized, bodies excluded, initializers dropped).

Snapshot: `tests/snapshots/physis_core_api.txt` — **2213 items** (64 top-level
`pub mod`, fields, variants, trait + inherent methods with full type paths).
Zero `?` names, zero over-400-char lines (bodies/initializers excluded).

Method limits (documented in the test header): macro-generated items appear
at their declaration site only; `pub(crate)` is correctly out of scope;
`#[doc(hidden)]` items are recorded WITH a marker (count today: 0).
