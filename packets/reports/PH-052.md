## What surprised me

- The magnitude and cleanliness: `D` on real primes is ~12× the worst
  independence null, and the held-out half reproduces discovery within 3%.
- The π/θ vs ψ view correlation is 0.999 — as it must be, since both count the
  same primes. This makes the "representation-covariance" claim intentionally
  weak (specificity + out-of-sample stability, not independence across
  representations), which is exactly the honesty the packet's Never rule
  demanded.
