# Plant Operations Master Agreement — POMA-3.8B (rev. 2026-06)

## 1. Subject and scope
This Master Agreement governs predictive and preventive maintenance of the
presses, lathes and machining centres listed in Annex A, between the Plant
Operator and the Service Consortium. It replaces POMA-3.7A in full. All
service levels in this document are contractual; a breach is counted per
calendar week and compensated per Annex C schedule.

## 2. Vibration limits and monitoring cadence
Spindle and bearing vibration shall be sampled every 4 hours at constant
load. The alarm threshold is set at 2.6 mm/s RMS; when the measured value
exceeds the threshold the machine must be stopped within 8 working hours
and a diagnostic report filed. Machines above 4.2 mm/s are decommissioned
de facto and must not be restarted under any circumstance until the spindle
is replaced and a new baseline is certified by the consortium.

## 3. Lubrication regime
Centralised lubrication of slideways is mandatory every 500 operating hours
with type HL-46 oil. The operator shall record each lubrication event in the
ledger within 24 hours. Skipping a scheduled lubrication voids the service
level for that machine for the quarter.

## 4. Spare parts and provenance
Only certified spare parts with a traceable lot number may be installed.
The certificate of conformity must be archived for 10 years. Parts without
a lot number are not permitted on the shop floor, even provisionally.

## 5. Shift reporting
Every shift produces a report (Rapporto di turno) within 2 hours of shift
close, listing: produced units, scrap units, six-big-loss stop minutes,
and any anomaly flagged by the monitoring system. The report is binding;
late reports are a level-2 breach. Scrap above 8% on any machine triggers
a mandatory quality review within 3 working days.

## 6. Predictive maintenance service level
The consortium guarantees detection of a degrading bearing at least 5 days
before functional failure, on machines with a healthy baseline of 14 days
or more. Detection inside 2 days of failure is a level-1 breach with the
compensation schedule of Annex C. Remaining useful life projections are
indicative and do not extend the guarantee.

## 7. General operating clauses

### 7.1 Tool change discipline
Tool changes are performed only after a first-piece check. The first piece is measured; if it fails, the batch is quarantined and 100% inspection follows.

### 7.2 Oil handling
Used oil is collected in sealed drums and consigned to the certified disposer within 30 days. Mixing HL-46 with any other grade is not permitted.

### 7.3 Access control
Maintenance areas are badge-controlled. Contractors must be accompanied at all times; unaccompanied access is a level-3 breach.

### 7.4 Data retention
Vibration raw data is retained 5 years; shift reports 10 years; certificates 10 years. Deletion before the retention term is forbidden.

### 7.5 Calibration
Measuring instruments are calibrated every 12 months. An out-of-calibration instrument invalidates the measurements taken with it.

### 7.6 Night shifts
Night shifts operate with a reduced crew; the predictive watch continues unattended with automatic escalation to the on-call engineer.

### 7.7 Training
Operators receive grid-training refreshers every 6 months. A new operator may not run a press unsupervised before the first refresher.

### 7.8 Energy
Compressed-air leaks above 0.5 bar equivalent are logged as a loss. The leak survey runs monthly.

### 7.9 Documentation language
Operational documentation is maintained in Italian; translations into English are informative only.

### 7.10 Warranty
Repairs carry a 12-month warranty from acceptance. Repeat failure inside the warranty window restarts the warranty on the repaired assembly only.

### 7.11 Escalation
A level-1 breach unresolved after 48 hours escalates to the consortium director; a level-2 after 5 working days to the plant manager.

### 7.12 Audit
The operator may audit the consortium twice per year with 10 days notice. Extra audits require agreement.

## 9. Annex C — compensation schedule (extract)

Level-1 breach: 4,000 EUR per week of exposure.
Level-2 breach: 1,200 EUR per week of exposure.
Level-3 breach: written warning, 3rd in a quarter becomes level-2.
