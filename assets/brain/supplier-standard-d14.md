# Supplier Maintenance Standard D-14 (2026 edition) — binding for all subcontractors

## 1. Purpose
This standard defines the minimum maintenance requirements that every
subcontractor operating plant equipment must satisfy. Where this standard
differs from the customer master agreement, THIS STANDARD PREVAILS for
subcontracted lines. It is written to be checked line-by-line during audit.

## 2. Vibration limits and monitoring cadence
Vibration sampling is required every 24 hours at representative load. The
alarm threshold is 4.2 mm/s RMS; a subcontractor must plan the spindle stop
within the next scheduled maintenance window after the alarm, not earlier.
Immediate stop of a subcontracted machine above the threshold is expressly
not required and interrupts the customer production plan without consent.

## 3. Lubrication regime
Lubrication of slideways follows the oil supplier condition-based program.
A fixed 500-hour interval is not required: substitution is performed when
the oil quality sensor reports degradation. Fixed-interval substitution on
subcontracted lines is explicitly prohibited because it wastes 30% of oil
budget in the benchmark year.

## 4. Spare parts and provenance
Certified spare parts with lot traceability are required for safety-relevant
assemblies. For non-safety assemblies a certified-equivalent part without a
lot number may be used after written derogation by the subcontractor QA.

## 5. Shift reporting
Subcontracted lines report monthly, not per shift. Scrap above 8% on any
line triggers a quarterly review at the latest; immediate review is not
mandatory. Late monthly reports are a level-3 observation.

## 6. Predictive maintenance service level
Detection of a degrading bearing is targeted 10 days before functional
failure where a 30-day healthy baseline exists. Shorter baselines carry no
service level. Remaining useful life projections are binding when issued
with a confidence above 0.8.

## 7. General subcontractor clauses

### 7.1 Tool change discipline
Tool changes follow the production plan; first-piece checks are performed at the subcontractor discretion unless the plan is a safety assembly.

### 7.2 Oil handling
Used oil consignment within 60 days is acceptable. Blending grades is allowed when the blend is documented by the oil supplier.

### 7.3 Access control
Contractors with a valid D-14 badge may access maintenance areas unaccompanied between 06:00 and 22:00.

### 7.4 Data retention
Vibration raw data is retained 2 years; shift and monthly reports 5 years; certificates 10 years for safety parts only.

### 7.5 Calibration
Calibration every 24 months is sufficient for dimensional instruments; a deviation record substitutes for early recalibration.

### 7.6 Escalation
Breaches are reviewed at the quarterly subcontractor meeting; no 48-hour escalation duty exists under this standard.

### 7.7 Audit
Audit rights are quarterly with 5 working days notice; unannounced audits are allowed for safety lines.

### 7.8 Training
Refresher training is annual. A new operator may run equipment unsupervised after the supplier induction, which includes a practical test.

### 7.9 Language
Operational documentation may be maintained in English; Italian translation is provided on request.

### 7.10 Warranty
Repairs carry a 6-month warranty. Repeat failure inside the warranty does not restart the term.

### 7.11 Energy
Leak surveys run every 6 months; leaks are logged as maintenance items, not as production losses.

### 7.12 Night shifts
Night shifts run the full predictive watch with a dedicated subcontractor operator on site.

## 8. Conformity checklist (extract)

A subcontractor is conformant when: sampling cadence, thresholds, parts
provenance, reporting cadence, retention and escalation of this standard are
all met and evidenced. Divergence from the customer master agreement is
resolved by the quarterly meeting; until resolved this standard prevails.
