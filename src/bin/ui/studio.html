Ontology studio

A pack is a dictionary of terms your business already uses, each filed on one of the 70 meaning cells. Classification scores text against every entry's hints, so hints are the words your people actually write — not synonyms of the entry's own name.
Try it classifies your sample text twice: once with the dictionary the engine is running now, once with your draft on its own. That comparison is live and costs nothing — it builds a throwaway classifier and throws it away. The draft column is not a preview of the merged result: every cell your draft leaves empty still carries the grid's generic anchor, so text your terms do not cover lands on an anchor rather than nowhere.
Save writes the pack to disk. It becomes part of classification at the next restart, and the pack list says which packs the running process actually has. Nothing here silently reclassifies your history.
