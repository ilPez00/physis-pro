Why this is a brain multiplier

A 3.8B-parameter model alone cannot know your contracts. Physis gives it retrieval (one shared embedding space), a language floor (the corpus's own n-gram tables — the same trick behind Qwen-3's ngram tables, here built in ~1 s from your documents), and a grounding gate (a verbatim quote that does not token-match the source is rejected, never shown). With the small local model you get fluent grounded answers with zero cloud; without it you still get exact verbatim answers — the page below is honest about which one spoke.
