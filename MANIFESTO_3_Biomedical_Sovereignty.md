## 2. The Data Architecture of Biomedical Sovereignty

The central thesis: you cannot improve what you cannot measure, and you cannot measure what your data architecture does not support. The standard approach — sporadic doctor visits, sporadic blood tests, population-reference ranges — is insufficient for individual sovereignty. Population ranges are not individual truth.

The praxisweb.xyz data architecture has three layers:

### Layer 1: Continuous Instrumentation
Wearable sensors (heart rate variability, resting HR, SpO₂, skin temperature), daily physiological baselines (weight, blood pressure, resting metrics), and symptom tracking. This is not "quantified self" as hobbyist project — it is the data feed that the architecture operates on.

### Layer 2: The Personal Data Protocol (praxisweb.xyz)
This is the software diary layer. Every dietary choice, every training session, every medication state change, every sleep cycle — all recorded with the same rigor as code commits. The protocol is:
- **Total transparency**: no hidden fields, no opaque algorithms over your data
- **Versioned records**: every state change is a committed entry with timestamp, value, and context
- **Exportable**: the entire data history can be dumped, analysed, imported elsewhere
- **Zero vendor lock-in**: data format is plain-text, machine-parseable, unencumbered

### Layer 3: The Feedback Engine
This is where the architecture drives action. The three principles:

#### Principle A: Causal Chaining
Not correlation — causal chaining. If removing psychiatric medication X is followed by physiological effect Y within Z time window, and Y is tracked in the diary, the connection is explicit. Not "maybe this helped"; "here is the recorded chain of events."

#### Principle B: Threshold-Gated Intervention
No arbitrary dose changes. Interventions are gated by measured thresholds:
- Weight dropping below X kg → reassess training load
- HRV dropping below Y bpm → reassess stress exposure
- Medication-free days reaching Z → reassess next reduction step

The protocol does not recommend changes. It reports thresholds crossed. The sovereign individual decides.

#### Principle C: Physiological First, Psychological Second
The body's physiological state precedes and constrains the psychological state. You cannot "think" your way out of a cortisol spike or a metabolic imbalance. The data architecture surfaces this: when physiological metrics move in a direction inconsistent with the psychological narrative, the architecture flags the inconsistency. The narrative is adjusted — not by edict, but by evidence.

## 3. The Mathematics of Reversal

The 45kg weight loss and psychiatric medication reversal are not "lifestyle changes" in the popular sense. They are engineered outcomes of the data architecture.

### The Energy Balance Equation (with tracking)
Standard advice: "eat less, move more." This is a zero-information statement — it is always true but never actionable.

The tracked approach:
```
Δweight = f(calories_in - calories_out, macronutrient_partitioning, sleep_quality, stress_hormones, medication_state)
```

Each variable is measured, not guessed. `calories_in` is not "I ate a salad" — it is the recorded intake from the diary, validated against weight change. `calories_out` is not "I was active" — it is the basal metabolic rate measured through daily weight + body composition tracking, corrected for sleep quality and stress hormones.

When each term in the equation is instrumented, the function becomes invertible. You can solve for the intervention that produces the desired Δweight. This is not magic. It is algebra with measured inputs.

### The Medication Withdrawal Protocol
Psychiatric medication withdrawal is framed not as "stopping pills" but as a structured data transition:

1. **Baseline establishment**: 90 days of full medication + continuous instrumentation. All physiological metrics recorded at baseline.
2. **Step reduction**: Each medication reduction step is gated by threshold conditions:
   - Physiological metrics stable for ≥30 days at current dose
   - No adverse trend in HRV, resting HR, blood pressure
   - Psychological symptom score (tracked daily) within ±1 of baseline
3. **Post-step monitoring**: 60 days of intensified tracking after each step. If any metric trends adverse, pause and stabilize before next step.
4. **Completion**: Medication-free state confirmed when all physiological metrics have stabilized in the expected direction (corrected for natural variation).

This is not a standard tapering schedule (which is dose-time linear). It is a data-gated protocol: the data decides when the next step happens, not the calendar.
